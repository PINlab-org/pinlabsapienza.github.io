# PINlab

PINlab has four main research directions.

**Computer vision and machine learning for perception.**

We work on methods that endow machines and robots with visual perception of
the environment and the intelligence to reason about it and take decisions.
Our research covers detection, recognition, re-identification and forecasting.

**Distributed and multi-agent intelligent systems.**

We study perception and intelligence in settings where multiple agents,
robots, sensors or people interact, with a focus on robust reasoning and
decision making in dynamic environments.

**General intelligence and learning.**

We investigate reasoning, meta-learning and domain adaptation to build models
that generalize across tasks, data distributions and application scenarios.

**Sustainable and interpretable AI.**

We develop low-power and constrained-resource approaches for sensors and
devices, with an emphasis on interpretable and verifiable artificial
intelligence.
